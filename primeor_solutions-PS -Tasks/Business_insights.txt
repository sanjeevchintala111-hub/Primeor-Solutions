# Task 3: Business Insights – E-Commerce Data Analysis

## 1. Top 10 Profitable Products

**Finding:**
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier generated the highest total profit of 25,199.94.
- Cisco and Motorola smartphones also appeared among the top profitable products.
- Technology products showed strong representation in the top 10.

**Business Insight:**

Technology products are important contributors to business profitability. The Canon imageCLASS copier is the highest-profit product in the analysis.

**Recommendation:**

Maintain sufficient inventory of high-profit products and monitor their pricing, demand, and stock availability.

---

## 2. Top 10 Customers by Sales

**Finding:**
- Tom Ashbrook generated the highest sales of 40,489.
- Tamara Chand ranked second with 37,453.
- Greg Tran ranked third with 35,552.

**Business Insight:**

A group of high-value customers contributes significantly to sales revenue. Retaining these customers can help maintain consistent business performance.

**Recommendation:**

Introduce customer loyalty programs, personalized offers, and targeted promotions to improve customer retention.

---

## 3. Region-Wise Total Sales

**Finding:**
- Central recorded the highest sales of 2,833,115.
- South ranked second with 1,600,600.
- North generated 1,248,121.
- Canada recorded the lowest sales of 66,932.

**Business Insight:**

Sales performance varies considerably across regions. Central is the strongest sales region, while Canada contributes the least.

**Recommendation:**

Maintain strong operations in Central and investigate opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing regions.

---

## 4. Category-Wise Average Profit

**Finding:**
- Technology recorded the highest average profit of 65.43.
- Furniture recorded an average profit of 29.00.
- Office Supplies recorded the lowest average profit of 16.57.

**Business Insight:**

Technology generates the highest average profit per record among the three product categories, while Office Supplies has comparatively lower average profitability.

**Recommendation:**

Review the pricing, costs, and discount strategies of Office Supplies to identify opportunities to improve profitability.

---

## 5. Average Discount by Category

**Finding:**
- Furniture received the highest average discount of 16.80.
- Office Supplies recorded an average discount of 13.74.
- Technology received the lowest average discount of 13.53.

**Business Insight:**

Furniture products receive higher average discounts than other categories. High discount levels may affect profit margins.

**Recommendation:**

Review Furniture discount policies and evaluate whether discounts are generating enough additional sales to justify their impact on profitability.

---

## 6. Orders with Negative Profit

**Finding:**
- The Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print recorded the highest individual loss in the results, at -6,599.98.
- Other products, including Motorola smartphones and printing equipment, also recorded substantial losses.

**Business Insight:**

Some products generate negative profit despite being sold. These losses may affect overall business profitability.

**Recommendation:**

Investigate product costs, selling prices, discount levels, and shipping expenses to identify the reasons behind negative-profit orders.

---

## 7. Monthly Sales Trend

**Finding:**
- December 2014 recorded the highest monthly sales of 503,154.
- January 2011 recorded the lowest monthly sales of 98,902.
- Overall monthly sales increased from 2011 to 2014.
- November and December generally showed strong sales performance.

**Business Insight:**

The business experienced an overall upward sales trend during the four-year period. Sales also showed seasonal fluctuations, with strong performance toward the end of the year.

**Recommendation:**

Analyze seasonal demand and prepare sufficient inventory and resources before high-sales months, especially November and December.

---

## 8. Market-Wise Revenue Analysis

**Finding:**
- APAC generated the highest revenue of 18,229,276.
- EU ranked second with 14,836,996.
- US generated 11,487,816.
- Canada recorded the lowest revenue of 237,288.

**Business Insight:**

APAC is the strongest revenue-generating market, followed by EU and US. Revenue contribution differs significantly across markets.

**Recommendation:**

Maintain growth in APAC and EU while identifying opportunities to improve revenue in lower-performing markets, especially Canada.

---

## 9. Top-Performing Sub-Categories

**Finding:**
- Phones generated the highest sales of 1,706,642.
- Copiers ranked second with 1,508,370.
- Chairs ranked third with 1,500,930.
- Binders recorded the lowest sales among the selected top 10, at 461,842.

**Business Insight:**

Phones, Copiers, and Chairs are the strongest sales-generating sub-categories in the analysis.

**Recommendation:**

Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing sub-categories and investigate the sales performance of lower-performing sub-categories to identify improvement opportunities.

---

## 10. Ship Mode Usage

**Finding:**
- Standard Class recorded the highest usage with 30,764 orders.
- Second Class recorded 10,304 orders.
- First Class recorded 7,504 orders.
- Same Day recorded the lowest usage with 2,699 orders.

**Business Insight:**

Most orders use Standard Class shipping. Customers use faster delivery options less frequently.

**Recommendation:**

Maintain efficient Standard Class shipping operations and evaluate customer demand and costs before expanding faster delivery options.

---

## Overall Business Conclusion

The SQL analysis of the e-commerce dataset reveals several important business patterns:

1. **Product Profitability:** Technology products, particularly Canon copiers, are important profit contributors.
2. **Customer Performance:** High-value customers such as Tom Ashbrook contribute significantly to sales.
3. **Regional Performance:** Central is the leading region by sales.
4. **Category Performance:** Technology has the highest average profit.
5. **Discount Strategy:** Furniture receives the highest average discount and deserves further margin analysis.
6. **Loss Management:** Certain products generate substantial negative profit and require investigation.
7. **Sales Growth:** Monthly sales show an overall upward trend from 2011 to 2014.
8. **Market Performance:** APAC leads the markets in total revenue.
9. **Product Demand:** Phones, Copiers, and Chairs are the leading sub-categories by sales.
10. **Shipping Preferences:** Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping method.

### Final Recommendation

The business should focus on improving product profitability, retaining valuable customers, optimizing discount strategies, strengthening lower-performing markets, and maintaining inventory for high-demand products. Regular monitoring through SQL reports and Power BI dashboards can support better business decisions.
